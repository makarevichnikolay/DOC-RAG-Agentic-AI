AMENDMENT No. 1 TO WAREHOUSE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment"), effective as of 1 June 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date"), is entered into between Weerts Logistic Park III NV, a company organized under the laws of Belgium (the "Landlord"), and Supplies Distributors SA, a company organized under the laws of Belgium (the "Tenant"), and amends the Warehouse Lease Agreement between the parties dated 1 September 2020 (the "Lease").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties entered into the Lease for warehouse and office premises in the Weerts Logistic Park;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to record an agreed adjustment of the rent indexation mechanism and an extension of the leased office area;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. RENT INDEXATION. With effect from 1 September 2026, the annual indexation of the rent under Article 4 of the Lease shall be calculated on the basis of the Belgian health index (indice sante / gezondheidsindex) published for the month preceding the anniversary of the Commencement Date, in accordance with the statutory formula. The base rent rates of EUR 3.70 per m2 per month for warehouse space and EUR 7.50 per m2 per month for office space remain unchanged.

2. ADDITIONAL OFFICE AREA. As of 1 September 2026, the leased premises are extended by 240 m2 of additional office space on the first floor of Building C, at the office rate set out in Article 5 of the Lease. The Tenant's proportionate share of common charges under Article 8 shall be recalculated accordingly.

3. NO OTHER CHANGES. Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Lease, including the term and automatic renewal mechanism in Article 6 and the insurance obligations in Article 9, remain in full force and effect.

4. GOVERNING LAW. This Amendment is governed by Belgian law. The courts of Liege retain exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Amendment as of the Amendment Effective Date.
