AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") is made and entered into as of June 15, 2026, by and between BLUE HARBOR RETAIL, INC., a Delaware corporation ("Buyer"), and CASCADE CONSUMER GOODS LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Vendor").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Buyer and Vendor are parties to that certain Master Vendor Supply Agreement dated as of October 1, 2024 (the "Agreement"); and

WHEREAS, the Parties desire to amend the Agreement to adjust Product pricing and to extend the non-renewal notice period, on the terms set forth herein;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. PRICE ADJUSTMENT. Effective for all Purchase Orders issued on or after September 1, 2026, the Prices set forth on Exhibit A (Product Schedule) to the Agreement shall be increased by two and one-half percent (2.5%) across all Product categories (Categories A, B, and C). Except as so adjusted, the Prices shall remain firm for the first Renewal Term.

2. EXTENDED NON-RENEWAL NOTICE PERIOD. Section 2.2 of the Agreement is hereby amended such that, with respect to Renewal Terms commencing on or after October 1, 2027, the period for delivery of written notice of non-renewal shall be not less than ninety (90) days (rather than sixty (60) days) prior to the expiration of the then-current Renewal Term. For the avoidance of doubt, the sixty (60) day notice period shall continue to apply to the Renewal Term commencing October 1, 2026.

3. NO OTHER CHANGES. Except as expressly amended hereby, all terms and conditions of the Agreement remain unmodified and in full force and effect. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

4. GOVERNING LAW. This Amendment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles, consistent with Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

5. COUNTERPARTS. This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Amendment as of the date first written above.
