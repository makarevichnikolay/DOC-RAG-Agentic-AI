RETAIL STORE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Retail Store Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is made and entered into as of March 1, 2021, by and between HARBORVIEW PROPERTIES LP, a New Hampshire limited partnership with an address at 55 Congress Street, Suite 400, Portsmouth, New Hampshire 03801 ("Landlord"), and BLUE HARBOR RETAIL, INC., a Delaware corporation with an address at 200 Commerce Way, Portsmouth, New Hampshire 03801 ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES

1.1 Demise. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, that certain retail unit consisting of approximately twelve thousand four hundred (12,400) square feet of gross leasable area, known as Unit 7 of the Harborview Commons Shopping Center, located at 740 Islington Street, Portsmouth, New Hampshire 03801 (the "Premises"), as more particularly shown on the site plan attached hereto as Exhibit A.

1.2 Common Areas. Tenant and its customers, employees, and invitees shall have the non-exclusive right to use the parking areas, sidewalks, driveways, and other common areas of the Shopping Center (the "Common Areas"), subject to reasonable rules and regulations promulgated by Landlord.

2. TERM; RENEWAL OPTION

2.1 Initial Term. The initial term of this Lease shall be seven (7) years, commencing on March 1, 2021 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on February 29, 2028 (the "Expiration Date"), unless sooner terminated in accordance with the terms hereof (the "Initial Term").

2.2 Lease Year. "Lease Year" means each successive twelve (12) month period during the Term commencing on the Commencement Date or an anniversary thereof.

2.3 Renewal Option. Provided that Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable notice and cure period, Tenant shall have one (1) option to extend the Term for an additional period of five (5) years (the "Renewal Term"), commencing immediately upon the expiration of the Initial Term. Tenant may exercise the Renewal Option only by delivering written notice of exercise to Landlord not less than nine (9) months prior to the Expiration Date (that is, on or before May 31, 2027). Base Rent during the Renewal Term shall continue to escalate as provided in Section 3.2. If Tenant fails to timely deliver such notice, the Renewal Option shall lapse and be of no further force or effect, time being of the essence.

3. BASE RENT

3.1 Base Rent. Commencing on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay to Landlord base rent ("Base Rent") at the initial annual rate of Twenty-Eight Dollars and Fifty Cents ($28.50) per square foot of gross leasable area, being Three Hundred Fifty-Three Thousand Four Hundred Dollars ($353,400.00) per annum, payable in equal monthly installments of Twenty-Nine Thousand Four Hundred Fifty Dollars ($29,450.00), in advance, on or before the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or setoff.

3.2 Escalation. Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3%) on the first day of each Lease Year following the first Lease Year, compounded annually, throughout the Initial Term and, if exercised, the Renewal Term.

3.3 Late Charge. If any installment of Rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to five percent (5%) of the overdue amount, plus interest on the overdue amount at the rate of twelve percent (12%) per annum until paid.

4. PERCENTAGE RENT

4.1 Percentage Rent. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay to Landlord, for each Lease Year, percentage rent ("Percentage Rent") equal to four percent (4%) of the amount by which Tenant's Gross Sales for such Lease Year exceed the Breakpoint. The "Breakpoint" for each Lease Year shall be the natural breakpoint, being the annual Base Rent for such Lease Year divided by four percent (4%).

4.2 Gross Sales. "Gross Sales" means the gross selling price of all merchandise and services sold in, at, or from the Premises, whether for cash or credit, excluding (a) sales, use, and excise taxes collected from customers and remitted to taxing authorities; (b) bona fide refunds and exchanges; (c) sales of fixtures and equipment not in the ordinary course; and (d) employee sales at discount, not to exceed two percent (2%) of Gross Sales.

4.3 Reporting. Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord a statement of Gross Sales for such quarter. Within ninety (90) days after the end of each Lease Year, Tenant shall deliver an annual statement of Gross Sales certified by an officer of Tenant, accompanied by payment of any Percentage Rent due for such Lease Year. Landlord may audit Tenant's Gross Sales records not more than once per Lease Year upon fifteen (15) days' notice; if an audit reveals an understatement of Gross Sales of more than three percent (3%), Tenant shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit.

5. COMMON AREA MAINTENANCE; TAXES

5.1 CAM Charges. Tenant shall pay to Landlord, as additional rent, Tenant's Proportionate Share of all costs incurred by Landlord in operating, maintaining, repairing, insuring, and managing the Common Areas and the Shopping Center ("CAM Charges"), including snow removal, landscaping, parking lot maintenance, exterior lighting, common utilities, property insurance, and a management fee not to exceed five percent (5%) of gross receipts of the Shopping Center. "Tenant's Proportionate Share" means 8.9%, being the ratio of the gross leasable area of the Premises to the total gross leasable area of the Shopping Center.

5.2 Estimated Payments; Reconciliation. Tenant shall pay estimated CAM Charges monthly with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver to Tenant a reconciliation statement, and any overpayment or underpayment shall be credited or paid, as applicable, within thirty (30) days.

5.3 CAM Cap. Controllable CAM Charges (excluding snow removal, insurance, and utilities) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per calendar year on a cumulative, compounding basis.

5.4 Real Estate Taxes. Tenant shall pay Tenant's Proportionate Share of all real estate taxes and assessments levied against the Shopping Center, payable monthly as additional rent based on Landlord's reasonable estimate, subject to annual reconciliation.

6. USE; OPERATING COVENANT

6.1 Permitted Use. The Premises shall be used solely for the retail sale of housewares, home goods, apparel, outdoor living products, and related merchandise, and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

6.2 Operating Hours. Tenant shall continuously operate its business in the Premises during at least the minimum hours of 10:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday through Saturday, and 11:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on Sunday, holidays excepted.

6.3 Exclusive. Landlord shall not lease any other unit in the Shopping Center in excess of 5,000 square feet to a tenant whose primary business is the retail sale of housewares, so long as Tenant is operating in the Premises and is not in default.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 Landlord Obligations. Landlord shall maintain, repair, and replace, as necessary, the roof, foundation, structural elements, and exterior walls of the Premises, and shall operate and maintain the Common Areas in good condition.

7.2 Tenant Obligations. Tenant shall, at its expense, maintain the interior non-structural portions of the Premises in good order and repair, including storefront glass, interior walls, floor coverings, ceilings, lighting, plumbing fixtures, and the HVAC unit exclusively serving the Premises (subject to a replacement cost amortization if replacement is required in the final two years of the Term).

8. ALTERATIONS; SIGNAGE

8.1 Tenant shall not make structural alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Non-structural interior alterations costing less than Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000) in any Lease Year shall not require consent, provided Tenant gives Landlord prior notice.

8.2 Tenant may install signage on the storefront and on the Shopping Center pylon sign, subject to Landlord's approval of design and compliance with applicable ordinances of the City of Portsmouth.

9. INSURANCE

9.1 Tenant's Insurance. Tenant shall maintain, at its expense, throughout the Term: (a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence and Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000) in the aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured; (b) special form property insurance covering Tenant's fixtures, inventory, and improvements at full replacement cost; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and (d) business interruption insurance covering at least twelve (12) months of Rent.

9.2 Certificates. Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the coverage required by Section 9.1 upon the Commencement Date and, thereafter, evidence of renewal not later than September 15 of each calendar year during the Term. Each policy shall provide that it may not be cancelled or materially modified without thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Landlord.

9.3 Landlord's Insurance. Landlord shall maintain special form property insurance on the Shopping Center at full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas, the costs of which shall be included in CAM Charges.

9.4 Waiver of Subrogation. Each party waives all rights of recovery against the other for loss or damage to the extent covered by property insurance required to be carried hereunder, and shall cause its insurers to waive rights of subrogation accordingly.

10. INDEMNITY

10.1 Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from and against all claims, damages, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises or the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents, or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord. Landlord shall likewise indemnify Tenant with respect to the Common Areas and Landlord's negligence or willful misconduct.

11. DAMAGE; DESTRUCTION; CONDEMNATION

11.1 If the Premises are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Premises with reasonable diligence, and Rent shall abate proportionately during the period of untenantability. If restoration cannot reasonably be completed within one hundred eighty (180) days, either party may terminate this Lease upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

11.2 If all or a material portion of the Premises is taken by eminent domain, this Lease shall terminate as of the date of taking, and the award shall belong to Landlord, except that Tenant may pursue a separate award for relocation expenses and the value of its trade fixtures.

12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but upon prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an affiliate of Tenant or to a successor by merger or acquisition of substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided the successor has a tangible net worth of not less than Tenant's as of the date hereof. No assignment or subletting shall release Tenant from liability hereunder.

13. DEFAULT; REMEDIES

13.1 Events of Default. Each of the following is an "Event of Default": (a) failure to pay Rent within five (5) days after written notice of nonpayment; (b) failure to perform any other obligation within thirty (30) days after written notice (or such longer period as reasonably necessary if cure is commenced and diligently pursued); (c) abandonment of the Premises; or (d) Tenant's bankruptcy or insolvency.

13.2 Remedies. Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease, re-enter and relet the Premises, and recover from Tenant all Rent due and other damages permitted by law, subject to Landlord's duty to mitigate.

14. GOVERNING LAW

14.1 This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New Hampshire, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts sitting in Rockingham County, New Hampshire, for any dispute arising under this Lease.

15. MISCELLANEOUS

15.1 Quiet Enjoyment. So long as Tenant is not in default beyond applicable cure periods, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly hold and enjoy the Premises for the Term.

15.2 Subordination; Estoppel. This Lease is subject and subordinate to any mortgage now or hereafter encumbering the Shopping Center, provided the holder thereof delivers a commercially reasonable non-disturbance agreement. Each party shall deliver an estoppel certificate within fifteen (15) days of request.

15.3 Holdover. If Tenant remains in possession after the expiration of the Term without Landlord's consent, Tenant shall be a tenant at sufferance at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent last in effect.

15.4 Notices. Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally recognized overnight courier, or certified mail (return receipt requested) to the addresses set forth in the preamble.

15.5 Entire Agreement. This Lease, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the Premises and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

15.6 Brokers. Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker other than Seacoast Commercial Realty, whose commission shall be paid by Landlord pursuant to a separate agreement.
